## 2. Founding story and ownership

- Founded in 1987 by two chefs, Fred Bramhall and Mary Clark, "who, in combination with partner Robert Lederman, have over sixty years combined experience"; "Now the leading bakery in Colorado with a full line of handmade products… natural ingredients… signature breads". Label: Confirmed (old site). Source: 2009 aboutUs.htm on Wayback, http://web.archive.org/web/2009/http://www.bluepointbakery.com/aboutUs.htm ; also the 2010 price list PDF (myonlinebakery.com/new/css_acc/images/bakeries/935_1289247151.pdf).
- 2014 About page: "founded in 1987 when the owners discovered a need for fine baked goods to service the restaurant industry… principals have over a century of combined restaurant and bakery experience, including a third generation baker." Confirmed (old site). Wayback 2014 about.html.
- The Bluepoint "empire" once included two Bluepoint restaurants and a retail bakery on Sixth Avenue (half of that space later became Fruition), plus the wholesale bakery, "huge, with contracts stretching from Colorado Springs to Fort Collins… restaurants, hotels, coffee shops, catering operations, airport concessionaires." Mary Clark: "I'm not a baker… the bakery is so big now that being an owner there is more like being a CEO." Confirmed. Westword, Jason Sheehan, "Market Watch", Mar 20, 2008: https://www.westword.com/food-drink/market-watch-5098125/
- Once a top retail bakery on Sixth Avenue. Confirmed. https://www.westword.com/location/bluepoint-bakery-5170644/
- Westword Best of Denver 2000: Best Bakery. Confirmed. https://www.westword.com/news/best-of-denver-winners-from-2000-5834471/
- Clark & Bramhall are listed as chef/co-owners from 1987, with Robert Lederman as partner. Probable (aggregator). https://www.chefdb.com/pl/7705/Bluepoint-Bakery-Denver
- Robert (Rob) Lederman: third-generation baker from a Worcester, MA family bake shop; owned a Florida bakery from 1976; 14 years in Publix bakery management; hired in 1991 by Bramhall & Clark as baker/manager; later a partner. Confirmed (article date unknown). https://www.ijn.com/at-rodef-shalomhamantaschen/
- BBB lists Robert Lederman as owner/manager (file opened 7/12/2002). Confirmed (BBB data, possibly outdated).
- Willie Ramirez worked in and managed Bluepoint for 28 years, then acquired 100% ownership with a $2,675,000 SBA loan, using his partial ownership as equity. All employees and the operation were kept intact, with "family members that surround him in the bakery every day." Quote: "Now that I own 100% of the bakery, I can focus on growing the business to meet the needs of my customers, namely providing the best breads to the best restaurants in Colorado." Confirmed (lender case study). Date Unknown (Probable 2018–2020). https://www.44businesscapital.com/business-loan-story/from-baker-to-manager-to-owner-long-time-employee-acquires-bakery-business-with-2675000-sba-loan/
- Guillermo Ramirez (SOS registered agent) is the same person as Willie Ramirez. Probable.
- "Ricky" is a co-owner or leader alongside Willie. Probable: a 2022 review names "Willie/Ricky", and the Chamber listing uses ricky@ email. https://exa.ai/library/place/m2vtnklj5mj
- "Proud, minority-owned and family-owned." Confirmed as a site claim (About page). Formal minority-business certification (e.g., MBE): Unknown, none found.

Whitespace read: in 2026 two local wholesale suppliers left the market (Aspen, Hinman), and Grateful Bread went distributor-only. The artisans are small and metro-only. Harvest Moon is buns plus national; izzio is frozen/par-baked plus national. Few independents still offer a full line (bread, buns, pastry, dessert), on their own trucks, six days a week, from Cheyenne to Fountain to Breckenridge, scratch-baked locally, owner-operated. Bluepoint appears to be one of them. Label: Probable. Their exact current route coverage is Unknown.
