Nine pixel-preserving source crop references have been created from these four original Bluepoint photos. Each has a source URL, normalized crop coordinates, possible product ID, and uncertainty note in the companion Pastries Batch 01 archive manifest.

The crops retain Bluepoint's original checked-cloth/rack environment: these are reference masters, not approved studio cutouts. The cream color #f6f1e8 is only an external review mat in this batch.

## Product integrity and QA

- Preserve the distinctive rounded horseshoe croissant shape; reject generic pointed-crescent concept art.
- Three visible 3 oz croissants are three specimens of the same product, not three SKUs.
- Danish fillings need product identification before labeling Apple or Cherry; do not substitute a round Danish for Bear Claw.
- The rear turnover does not establish an Apple filling.
- Rectangular chocolate-drizzled pastries should not automatically be sold as a Chocolate Croissant.
- Final assets need consistent cream background, realistic soft shadow, crop fidelity and no neighboring-product fragments.
- Distinguish directly edited Bluepoint photos from generated representative artwork with accurate provenance and labels.
