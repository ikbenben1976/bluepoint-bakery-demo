## Distinguish three kinds of evidence

- **Photo shows product**: confirmed visually; image may contain multiple pieces but not necessarily distinct SKUs.
- **Specific SKU match**: supported by original filename, menu and visible details; still needs confirmation before claiming the photo shows the exact product and size.
- **Search-only thumbnail**: other search-engine results; not automatically a Bluepoint photograph or permission-cleared.

## E. Extraction constraints

- A group image featuring three pieces of the same product does not create three additional uniquely covered SKUs. Example: Golden Parisien, Brioche dinner rolls, 3 oz Croissants, Cherry turnovers, and 16×4 loaves.
- **Original binary archive is still pending verification.** A source-image collector workflow was added under `.github/workflows/collect-bluepoint-source-images.yml`; its artifact must be verified before claiming originals were downloaded.
- The HTML/source inventory covers original first-party pages and documented image URLs; a browser-level complete carousel crawl and public Vercel responsive visual audit remain incomplete.
- Existing `docs/PHOTOS.md` contains an older, useful provenance ledger; its note about `photos/originals/` is no longer reflected by the current repository tree. The GitHub Action preserves downloads as an ephemeral artifact rather than committing copyrighted originals to the website source.
- The user's image-search screenshots are **secondary verification**, not proof an image is a Bluepoint-produced photo. Keep official, external and generated imagery clearly distinguished.
