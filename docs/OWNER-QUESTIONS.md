Unconfirmed facts for the wholesale request demo. Each line is an issue, the question, and what the demo shows instead. Nothing below is stated as a Bluepoint policy.

- #30 | Are custom dimensions or pack formats available when they are not already in the catalog? | The size and packaging fields start blank and are not described as something the bakery can make.
- #30 | What is the price, minimum, or lead time for a customization? | The request does not show a price, a minimum, or an extra lead time for customization.
- #34 | When a catalog line says an item is available individually wrapped, is wrapping a choice on each order or the usual pack? | Where the catalog publishes individual wrapping and the pack size is not already a unit choice, the request has an unchecked box, “Mention individual wrapping.” It does not say the item arrives wrapped unless that box is checked.
- #35 | Which six days does Bluepoint deliver, and what is the weekday schedule for each route? | Answered 2026-11-05 from Ben’s distribution map. The delivery legend now shows Monday through Saturday (Sunday is not on the legend). Yellow runs all six days; green, blue, orange, and purple are subsets, listed under #46. The request form still lets a buyer list preferred days to discuss, including leaving them unselected, and does not treat a selection as a reserved stop.
- #36 | Does a ZIP code qualify a business for delivery? | A ZIP code is not treated as eligible. The field asks for a city.
- #36 | Does a published city or area name confirm the street address or how often the route runs? | A matching city is called a confirmed published area, with a link to the delivery map, and the note says the address and frequency are still confirmed by the bakery.

- #40 | What do samples cost, and how are they fulfilled? | The request says cost and fulfillment are not published and does not quote a fee.
- #40 | Which products can be sampled? | If the buyer asks the question, an optional note records products to ask about. It is not a sample menu.
