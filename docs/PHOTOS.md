# Photographs from bluepointbakery.com

Crawled once, politely, on 3 November 2026. Pages: home, about, breads, pastries, community, contact, FAQ, and the map page. The order portal and the payment portal were not opened.

Original files are in `photos/originals/`. The highest file the CDN actually returned is listed below. A name ending in `-1920w` is only the CDN’s label; several of those files are much smaller than 1920 pixels, and that is the largest copy they publish.

No stand-in from Unsplash, Pexels, or anywhere else is on the demo. Where a cutout failed, another of Bluepoint’s own photographs was used, or the bad part of the frame was left out.

The homepage hero is a still arrangement of five of those cutouts: marble rye, Golden Parisien, sandwich rolls, the sesame bun, and croissants. The caption says they are Bluepoint’s own photographs. No representative stock photo was added.
